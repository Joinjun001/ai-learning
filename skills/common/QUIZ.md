# Skill: Quiz

Use quizzes as evidence collection, not as punishment or repetition.

## Progression

1. recognition: identify the right idea
2. direct application: solve a standard case
3. explanation: state why the method works
4. transfer: handle a changed or unfamiliar case

## Adaptation

- A wrong answer should trigger diagnosis of the smallest missing dependency.
- A correct answer with weak reasoning may justify another targeted check.
- Repeated independent success is stronger evidence than self-reported confidence.
