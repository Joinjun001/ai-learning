# Skill: Orientation

Use when the learner is entering a concept with little or no context.

## Objective

Create a reason to learn the concept before introducing formal machinery.

## Procedure

1. Name the practical or theoretical problem.
2. Show what fails or becomes difficult without the concept.
3. State where the concept is used.
4. Give a one-sentence intuition.
5. Only then introduce terminology or notation.

Keep orientation short enough that it does not replace the actual lesson.
